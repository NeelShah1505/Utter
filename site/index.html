Features

Built for focus, not friction

Everything you need to dictate into any app. Nothing you don't.

Streaming transcription

Text appears as you speak — under 300ms latency on a mid-range laptop. Powered by Apple ANE on Apple Silicon, whisper.cpp on Intel and Windows.

Works completely offline

No network connection required. Core dictation runs on-device — audio never leaves your machine. Works on a plane, in a basement, anywhere.

No account required

Install and use immediately. No sign-up, no email, no subscription. No telemetry and no crash reporting — ever.

Optional AI cleanup

Off by default. Optionally route transcripts through a local Ollama instance, a remote Ollama server, or any OpenAI-compatible API to fix filler words and grammar.

Open source, MIT license

Full source available on GitHub. Inspect what runs on your machine, contribute improvements, or fork it for your own use — no restrictions.

How it works

Three steps, then just talk

No model downloads. No account setup. No configuration required.

01

Install

Download the installer for your platform. Run it. The app is roughly 10 MB and installs like any other desktop application.

02

Grant permissions

The first time you dictate, your OS will ask for microphone access. On macOS, Accessibility access is also needed to insert text. You grant both once, in system settings.

03

Press the hotkey and speak

Press Cmd+Shift+D (macOS) or Ctrl+Shift+D (Windows) from any application. Speak. Your words appear where your cursor is. Press again to stop.
