[ 01 ] HOW IT WORKS

THREE SECONDS,
START TO TYPED.

[ 01 ]

⌘⇧D

PRESS THE HOTKEY

From any app. A small bubble appears and starts listening. Your cursor never leaves the field you were already typing in.

[ 02 ]

SAY THE SENTENCE

Audio is processed by a Whisper model running locally on your machine. Nothing is uploaded. It works with Wi‑Fi off.

[ 03 ]

YOUR WORDS LAND

Press the hotkey again. The finished text is typed straight into Notes, Slack, your editor, the browser — wherever you were.

[ 02 ] WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

NOT A WRAPPER AROUND
DICTATION.

macOS already has a dictation key. Utter exists because the useful part isn't hearing the words — it's what happens to them next.

01

IT RUNS ON YOUR MACHINE

whisper.cpp, locally. The transcript is never sent anywhere unless you deliberately switch on the optional cleanup pass. Airplane mode changes nothing.

02

YOUR VOCABULARY, HEARD RIGHT

Add useState, kubectl, a colleague's name. Utter feeds them to the recognizer while it decodes — so they come out correct the first time, not patched afterwards.

03

OPTIONAL AI CLEANUP

Point it at a local Ollama or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. It strips filler, fixes grammar, and resolves corrections you make mid-sentence. Off by default.

04

YOURS TO READ AND CHANGE

MIT licensed, every line on GitHub. No sign-in wall, no usage cap, no "pro" tier holding the good part hostage.

[ 03 ] THE PART PEOPLE NOTICE

IT FIXES HOW PEOPLE
ACTUALLY TALK.

Nobody speaks in clean prose. You change your mind halfway through a sentence. With cleanup on, Utter sorts that out instead of typing the mess.

INPUT · WHAT YOU SAID

“book an appointment for nine a.m. oh no sorry book an appointment for ten p.m.”

OUTPUT · WHAT UTTER TYPED

“Book an appointment for 10 p.m.”

Also handled: filler words, run-on grammar, and tone matched to the app you're writing in. The model is your choice — local or cloud.
