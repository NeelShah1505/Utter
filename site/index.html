01 How it works

Three seconds,
start to typed.

01

⌘⇧D

Press the hotkey

From any app. A small bubble appears and starts listening. Your cursor never leaves the text field you were in.

02

Say the sentence

Audio is processed by a Whisper model running locally on your machine. Nothing is uploaded. It works with Wi‑Fi off.

03

Your words land

Press the hotkey again. The finished text is typed straight into Notes, Slack, your editor, the browser — wherever you were.

02 Why it's different

Not a wrapper
around dictation.

macOS already has a dictation key. Utter exists because the useful part isn't hearing the words — it's what happens to them next.

It runs on your machine

whisper.cpp, locally. The transcript is never sent anywhere unless you deliberately turn on the optional cleanup pass. Airplane mode changes nothing.

Your vocabulary, heard correctly

Add useState, kubectl, a colleague's name. Utter feeds them to the recognizer as it decodes — so they come out right the first time, not patched afterwards.

Optional AI cleanup

Point it at a local Ollama or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint and it removes filler, fixes grammar, and resolves the corrections you make mid-sentence. Off by default.

Yours to read and change

MIT licensed, every line on GitHub. No sign-in wall, no usage cap, no "pro" tier holding the good part hostage.

03 The part people notice

It fixes the way
people actually talk.

Nobody speaks in clean prose. You change your mind halfway through a sentence. With cleanup on, Utter sorts that out instead of typing the mess.

You said

“book an appointment for nine a.m. oh no sorry book an appointment for ten p.m.”

Utter typed

“Book an appointment for 10 p.m.”

Also handled: filler words, run-on grammar, and tone that matches the app you're writing in. The model you point it at is your choice — local or cloud.
